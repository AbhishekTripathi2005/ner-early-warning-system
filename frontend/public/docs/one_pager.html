AI-Based Early Warning & Landslide Monitoring in NER

Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER) • Disaster Management Cell
Problem ID: SIH26001
Category: Software • 8 NER States

1. The Problem Statement & Regional Vulnerability

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India experiences severe monsoon cloudbursts (>260mm/48h) and high seismicity (Zone V), triggering over 150 catastrophic landslides annually. Critical lifelines—such as NH-10 (Sikkim Lifeline) and SH-5 (Meghalaya Sohra)—collapse repeatedly. Existing warning mechanisms fail due to district-level coarseness (15-25 km), total communication blackout during hill storm cable cuts, lack of localized ground truth verification, and zero road-isolation impact intelligence for rescue forces.

2. The Solution: A Cloud-Native & Field-Resilient Platform

🔬 Hybrid Physics + GeoAI Modeling
Couples slope mechanics (Dynamic Factor of Safety FoS) with XGBoost and PyTorch LSTMs, fusing Sentinel-1 InSAR surface displacement (-28mm/yr creep), IMD Doppler radar, and 30m Digital Elevation Models.
⚡ Offline-First PWA (Service Worker + IndexedDB)
Engineered for zero-network conditions. Map tiles and alerts cache locally. Citizen hazard reports queue offline and auto-sync in the background the exact second network connectivity is restored.
📹 Crowdsourced Video & Photo Reporting
Enables citizens and field scouts to submit photos or short video clips (15-20s) with 1-click GPS coordinate auto-detection and automated AI spatial-correlation validation against GSI slope data.
🛣️ Road-Isolation Graph Intelligence
Derives cut-off Himalayan hamlets and affected populations for every blocked highway card (e.g. NH-10: 5 hamlets cut off • 18,400 residents isolated) with dynamic detour advisories.
📊 Explainable Multi-Horizon Weather Nowcast
Interactive Recharts dual-axis projection correlating Precipitation (mm/h) against Landslide Susceptibility (0.0-1.0) across 6h Nowcast, 24h Cumulative, and 48h Outlook.
📢 Inclusive Multilingual Voice Alerts
Features Web Audio evacuation siren synthesizers, NDMA CAP v1.2 SMS dispatches, Web Speech API spoken audio bulletins, and 5 languages (English, Hindi, Assamese, Bodo, Khasi).
