The Problem & Regional Vulnerability in North East India
Slide 2: Context

Geological & Climatic Extremes

  • World's highest rainfall (>11,000 mm/yr in Meghalaya) combined with young, fragile Himalayan tectonic folds (Seismic Zone V).
  • Over 150 catastrophic landslides occur every monsoon season, cutting off critical arterial corridors.
  • Single points of failure: NH-10 (Sikkim lifeline) and SH-5 (Sohra) frequently severed for days.

The 4 Existing System Failures

  • Coarse Scale: Regional generic district alerts (15-25 km) rather than road-kilometer accuracy.
  • The Last-Mile Blackout: Severe storms down cell towers and fiber lines; existing apps fail completely offline.
  • No Field Ground Truth: Satellite data is delayed 24-48 hours; zero crowdsourced citizen photos or videos.
  • Static Warnings: No road-isolation impact metrics for NDRF/SDRF tactical response.
Our Unique 7-Pillar Solution Architecture
Slide 3: Innovations

1. Physics + GeoAI

Couples Dynamic Factor of Safety (FoS) with XGBoost & PyTorch, Sentinel-1 InSAR (-28mm/yr creep), and IMD Doppler Radar.

2. Citizen Photo/Video

Crowdsourced hazard reporting with 1-click auto-GPS coordinate detection and video player popup on GIS map.

3. Road-Isolation Graph

Calculates cut-off Himalayan hamlets and affected populations for every blocked road card.

4. Offline-First PWA

Service Worker + IndexedDB caching keeps map tiles and alerts alive during total network blackout; auto-syncs on reconnect.

5. Multi-Horizon Nowcast

Recharts dual-axis projection correlating precipitation (mm/h) against Landslide Susceptibility across 6h, 24h, and 48h.

6. Continuous Retraining

Officer panel to mark alerts as Confirmed (TP), False Alarm (FP), or Missed Event (FN) to retrain AI pipeline.

Scalability Roadmap Across All 8 NER States
Slide 7: Scalability

State-Specific Risk Calibrations

  • Sikkim & Arunachal: GLOF and steep rockfall along NH-10 and Tawang lifelines.
  • Meghalaya: Escarpment saturation in Cherrapunji and Shillong plateau.
  • Assam & Tripura: Soft sedimentary hill cutting and railway line slips (Dima Hasao).
  • Mizoram & Nagaland: Urban slope creep and clay weathering (Aizawl & Kohima).

Zero-Cost Infrastructure Scaling

  • Free open satellite data (Sentinel-1/2, Copernicus 30m DEM, IMD Gridded).
  • Public watermark-free Esri Dark Gray canvas endpoints—zero recurring tile costs.
  • Containerized microservices ready for immediate deployment on NIC MeghRaj Cloud.
Enterprise Tech Stack & Open Standards
Slide 8: Tech Stack

Technology Components

  • Frontend: Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet, Recharts, Web Audio & Web Speech APIs.
  • Backend & GIS: Python FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL + PostGIS (EPSG:4326), Redis, Celery.
  • AI & ML: XGBoost, PyTorch (Temporal LSTM), Scikit-Learn.

Standards & Protocols

  • OGC (Open Geospatial Consortium) GeoJSON standard compliance.
  • NDMA Common Alerting Protocol (CAP v1.2) for multi-channel SMS broadcast.
  • W3C Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker specification.
